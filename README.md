# QRLF Whiteboard

**Words, silence, and being.**

QRLF Whiteboard is a faceless teaching project that explains QRLF Theory, created by Darwin Troy Ward, one idea at a time on a whiteboard. It looks at how the words we speak shape the field we live in, what we find in the silence between words, and what it means simply to be. Faith is the anchor.

QRLF is paired with a companion framework, **The Observed Reality Paradox (ORP)**.

QRLF and ORP are experiential and philosophical frameworks. They are not settled physics.
